## Description

The equation used by [`egfr()`](../egfr/) with `method = "CKDEPI 2021 cystatin"`.

## Details

The CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine-cystatin equation:
$$
eGFR = 135 \cdot \min(S_{cr}/\kappa, 1)^\alpha \cdot \max(S_{cr}/\kappa, 1)^{-0.544} \cdot \min(S_{cys}/0.8, 1)^{-0.323} \
\cdot \max(S_{cys}/0.8, 1)^{-0.778} \cdot 0.9961^A \cdot 0.963^F
$$


where:
- $S_{cr}$ = serum creatinine (mg/dL)
- $S_{cys}$ = serum cystatin C (mg/L)
- $\kappa$ = 0.7 (female) or 0.9 (male)
- $\alpha$ = -0.219 (female) or -0.144 (male)
- $A$ = age (years)
- $F$ = 1 (female) or 0 (male)
