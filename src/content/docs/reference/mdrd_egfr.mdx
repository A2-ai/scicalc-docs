## Details

The MDRD equation:
$$
eGFR = 175 \cdot S_{cr}^{-1.154} \cdot A^{-0.203} \cdot 0.742^F \cdot 1.212^B
$$


where:
- $S_{cr}$ = serum creatinine (mg/dL)
- $A$ = age (years)
- $F$ = 1 (female) or 0 (male)
- $B$ = 1 (Black) or 0 (other)






## Usage

```r
mdrd_egfr(sexf, raceb, age, creat)
```
