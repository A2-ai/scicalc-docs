Calculates Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate based on Sex, Race, Age, and Creatinine levels based on the CKDEPI 2009 equation

## Arguments
- `sexf` - Boolean, `TRUE` if subject is female
- `raceb` - Boolean, `TRUE` if subject is black
- `age` - Subject age $\text{year}$
- `creat` - Subject serum creatinine levels $\text{mg/dL}$

## Equation
Using $A$ for age

$$
\begin{aligned}
    K &= 
    \begin{cases} 
        0.7 & \text{if } \text{sexf is true}, \\
        0.9 & \text{otherwise},
    \end{cases} \\
    \alpha &= 
    \begin{cases} 
        -0.329 & \text{if } \text{sexf is true}, \\
        -0.411 & \text{otherwise},
    \end{cases} \\
    \text{sex\_mult} &= 
    \begin{cases} 
        1.018 & \text{if } \text{sexf is true}, \\
        1 & \text{otherwise},
    \end{cases} \\
    \text{race\_mult} &= 
    \begin{cases} 
        1.159 & \text{if } \text{raceb is true}, \\
        1 & \text{otherwise},
    \end{cases} \\
    \text{ratio} &= \frac{\text{creat}}{K}, \\
    \text{scr\_k} &= 
    \begin{cases} 
        \text{ratio}^\alpha & \text{if } \text{ratio} < 1, \\
        \text{ratio}^{-1.209} & \text{otherwise},
    \end{cases} \\
    \text{eGFR} &= 141 \cdot \text{scr\_k} \cdot (0.993^{\text{age}}) \cdot \text{sex\_mult} \cdot \text{race\_mult}.
\end{aligned}
$$
