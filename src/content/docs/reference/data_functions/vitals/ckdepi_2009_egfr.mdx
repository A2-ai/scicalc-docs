Calculates Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate based on Sex, Race, Age, and Creatinine levels based on the CKDEPI 2009 equation

## Arguments
- `sexf` - Boolean, `TRUE` if subject is female
- `raceb` - Boolean, `TRUE` if subject is black
- `age` - Subject age $\text{year}$
- `creat` - Subject serum creatinine levels $\text{mg/dL}$

## Equation
Using:
- $S_{cr}$ for serum creatinine levels
- $ \kappa = \begin{cases} 0.7 & \text{if sexf is True}\\ 0.9 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$
- $ \alpha = \begin{cases} -0.329 & \text{if } \text{sexf is true}\\-0.411 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$
- $A$ for age
- $ F = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if sexf is True}\\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$
- $ B = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if raceb is True}\\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$


$$
\text{eGFR} = 141 \cdot 
    \min(S_{cr}/\kappa, 1)^\alpha \cdot
    \max(S_{cr}/\kappa, 1)^{-1.209} \cdot
    0.993^A \cdot
    1.018^F \cdot
    1.159^B
$$

## Code
```r
K <- dplyr::if_else(sexf, 0.7, 0.9)
alpha <- dplyr::if_else(sexf, -0.329, -0.411)
sex_mult <- dplyr::if_else(sexf, 1.018, 1)
race_mult <- dplyr::if_else(raceb, 1.159, 1)

ratio <- creat/K
scr_k_min <- dplyr::if_else(ratio < 1, ratio^alpha, 1)
scr_k_max <- dplyr::if_else(ratio > 1, ratio^-1.209, 1)

egfr <- 141 *
  scr_k_min *
  scr_k_max *
  (0.993^age) *
  sex_mult *
  race_mult
```
## Units
This gives $\text{eGFR}$ in units of $\frac{\text{mL}}{\text{min} \cdot 1.73\text{m}^2}$
