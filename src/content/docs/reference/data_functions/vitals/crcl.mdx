Calculates Creatinine clearance with Cockcroft-Gault equation.

## Arguments
- `sexf` - Boolean, `TRUE` if subject is female otherwise FALSE.
- `age` - Age of subject in $\text{years}$
- `creat` - Subject serum creatinine levels in $\text{mg/dL}$
- `weight` - Subject weight in $\text{kg}$

## Equation

Using $A$ for Age, $W$ for Weight, $S_{cr}$ for serum Creatinine levels:
$$
\text{crcl} = f \cdot \frac{(140 - \text{A}) W}{72 S_{cr}}
$$ 
where
$$
f = 
\begin{cases} 
0.85 & \text{if female}, \\
1 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

```r
sex_mult <- ifelse(sexf, 0.85, 1)
crcl <- (140 - age) * weight / (72 * creat) * sex_mult
```

## Units
This gives creatinine clearance in $\text{mL/min}$
